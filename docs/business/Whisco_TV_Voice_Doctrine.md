# WHISCO TV — VOICE DOCTRINE v1.0
**Adopted 12 September 2026 · Source: first family/friends user-testing round (majority feedback) + Grok Design Vision pack · Governing for ALL copy: site, apps, posts, outreach, investor materials**

---

## 1. The two voices (visitor-validated, now law)

| | **WHISCO TV** (the company) | **WHISCO** (the dog) |
|---|---|---|
| Register | Professional, warm, precise. A real media company that respects the reader. | Quirky, family-safe, brief. The Shih Tzu who runs the station. |
| Surfaces | Homepage body, guides, /where pages, About (body), press kit, ALL investor/partner/legal materials, store listings, outreach ("— Ali, founder") | Empty states, loading, 404, greetings, his marked asides in articles, seasonal boards, social moments |
| Sample | "Every channel is verified before it appears — and re-verified around the clock." | "That page walked off." |
| Numbers | Floors + dated facts only | NEVER cites numbers |
| Marking | Unmarked (default voice) | Always visibly HIS: speech bubble, 🐾 marker, italic aside, or the mascot component — never mid-paragraph in company prose |

**The line:** the company informs, the dog charms. When one sentence tries to do both, it does neither. Visitors independently confirmed what the design pack ruled: "one greeting, then quiet."

## 2. The "defending free" rule (visitor finding #1)

Diagnosis: the homepage says "free / no catch / no signup" so often it reads as defensive — like a service arguing with an accusation nobody made. Confidence shows the content first.

**Rules:**
1. "Free" claims on any single page: **maximum 2** — one in the hero area ("Free. No catch." — the approved copy-deck line), one near the footer/CTA. Everything between lets the catalog speak.
2. Never stack synonyms ("100% free, no subscription, no credit card, no catch, ever" in one breath = the old App Store screenshot mistake in prose form).
3. The description of WHAT we have (600+ channels, 13 languages, tonight's picks) always outweighs the description of what we DON'T charge.
4. Store metadata already complies (Apple 2.3.7 scrub) — the site now follows the same discipline by choice.

## 3. Investor-grade polish rule (visitor finding #3, forward-looking)

- Investor, partner, licensor, and press materials: **company voice only.** Zero paw prints, zero dog jokes. (The dog may appear as brand imagery; he does not narrate business documents.)
- Site copy is presumed investor-visible at all times — a diligence associate WILL read the homepage. The polish bar is "would this embarrass us in a data room?"
- Typos, mixed scripts, wrong years, stretched thumbnails = diligence red flags dressed as small bugs. Hence the automated content-QA sweep (workflow: content-qa.yml, weekly) + fix-on-sight rule.
