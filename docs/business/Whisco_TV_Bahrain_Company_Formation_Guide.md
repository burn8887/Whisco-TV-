# 1. Entity comparison

## 1.1 The SPC question, answered plainly

**You cannot register a new SPC in Bahrain.** Legislative Decree No. 28 of 2020 repealed Part VIII of the Commercial Companies Law (Decree 21/2001). From 1 December 2020 every existing SPC was reclassified as a WLL. Formation houses still say “SPC” as marketing shorthand for a **single-shareholder WLL**. That is the live legal form.

A WLL may now have **one shareholder**. You do not need a nominee partner.

## 1.2 Four real options for Whisco TV

| | Stay unregistered | Sijili (virtual individual CR) | Individual Establishment (sole prop) | Single-shareholder WLL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who can use it | Anyone | **Bahraini national only**; not a public-sector employee; not already owner/manager of another CR (shareholder-only is OK) | Bahraini / GCC / US nationals | Anyone; 100% foreign ownership allowed on these activities |
| Legal personality | None. You *are* the business. | Individual CR. You *are* the business. | Individual CR. You *are* the business. | Separate legal person. |
| Liability | Unlimited personal | Unlimited personal | Unlimited personal | Limited to declared capital (subject to fraud / personal guarantees) |
| Physical address | None | Mailing address only | Usually required | Registered address required. Virtual office is accepted for services/tech. |
| Government setup | BHD 0 | **BHD 50** | ~BHD 80–200 | **~BHD 230–400 DIY government fees** |
| Annual government | 0 | ~BHD 27–50 | ~BHD 50–150 | CR BHD 50 + activity BHD 100 + BCCI BHD 8–16 + municipality ~BHD 10 |
| LMRA / visas | n/a | **No work permits allowed** | Possible if licensed as establishment | Possible once employer file opened. Not needed for a solo founder. |
| Bank account | Personal only | Some banks accept Sijili; mixed, often treated as personal/micro | Possible | Corporate IBAN. What Filmhub, AdSense (as company), Apple, Google want. |
| Sign Filmhub / content licences | As an individual. Counterparties resist. No asset ring-fence. | As an individual trader. Weak for US/EU licensors. | Same weakness. | **Yes — company is the contracting party.** IP can sit in the company. |
| AdSense / Play / App Store | Personal publisher. Workable today. Hard to transfer later. | Personal/Sijili payee. | Same. | Company payee + corporate bank. Cleaner KYC and tax trail. |
| Investor-readiness | None | None | None | Can issue new shares, convert to BSC(c), take a note. UBO already on file. |
| Time to live | Immediate | Hours to 3 working days | Hours to 3 days | ~5–15 working days if activities stay unregulated |
| Fit for Whisco | Bridge only | Cheap legal identity, wrong long-term vehicle | Wrong | **Recommended** |

## 1.3 Recommendation

**Incorporate a single-shareholder With Limited Liability company (W.L.L.) owned 100% by Ali Albaharna, trading as “Whisco TV”.**

Proposed registered name (three backups to reserve):

1. **Whisco Media W.L.L.** *(preferred — “Media” is accurate and less likely to be read as a broadcast licence than “Television”)*
2. Whisco Digital W.L.L.
3. Whisco Entertainment W.L.L.

Keep **Whisco TV** as the trading / brand / domain name. Do not put “Bahrain Radio and Television” style words in the registered name.

### Why not Sijili first

Sijili is tempting: BHD 50, no office, and the eligible list **does** include software publishing, computer programming, data hosting, web portals, and motion-picture/video/TV programme activities. It is the right tool for a freelancer selling design work. It is the wrong tool for Whisco because:

- Filmhub (and any later Cineverse / allrites deal) contracts with a **company**, wants a tax ID / CR, and will diligence limited liability and IP ownership.
- Liability is unlimited. One rights dispute or a payment-processor clawback lands on the founder personally.
- No work permits if you ever hire.
- Banks and app-store payouts treat it as a sole trader.
- You will incorporate a WLL within months anyway and then have to novate every contract and re-KYC AdSense / Apple / Google / the bank. Do the conversion once.

**Exception:** if a counterparty demands a CR *this week* and the WLL is still in notary queue, file Sijili as a 30-day bridge, then fold activity into the WLL and cancel Sijili. Do not sign Filmhub on the Sijili.

### Why not stay unregistered

The platform can keep operating. What it cannot do cleanly:

- Be the licensee on a Filmhub channel agreement.
- Open a corporate account that receives USD wires from Google, Apple, or a US marketplace.
- Give the angel a document that is not a handshake.
- Ring-fence the catalogue, brand, and code from personal creditors.

Formation cost is within the existing $200/month + $300 content/infra envelope if done DIY or with a lean local filer. Waiting is more expensive than the CR fee.

# 2. Activity codes — pick these, not a broadcast licence

Sijilat uses ISIC4. The first three codes cost BHD 100 together. Extra codes cost BHD 100 each. Start with three. Add later.

## 2.1 Recommended Day-1 bundle

| Priority | ISIC / Sijilat activity | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **6312 — Operations of eMarketplaces / websites / web portals** | This *is* Whisco TV: a website/app that organises and presents content. MOIC-only. You must enter https://whisco.tv in the dedicated Sijilat field. |
| 2 | **6201 — Computer programming activities** | Building and maintaining the web app, Android, iOS. MOIC-only. |
| 3 | **6311 — Data processing, hosting and related activities** | Catalog database, health-check automation, hosting. MOIC-only. |

These three describe the business without walking into a Ministry of Information queue on Day 1.

## 2.2 Add later, only if a counterparty or the ministry asks

| Code | When | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| 582 Software publishing | If you ship a paid or branded software product. Unlikely. | Usually MOIC-only. |
| **591 Motion picture, video and television programme activities** | If you produce or officially distribute programmes, or a licensor insists the CR lists audiovisual distribution. | On Sijili this is **MOIC + MIA (Publication and Press Directorate)**. On a WLL expect the same extra approval. Adds weeks. |
| 731 Advertising | If you sell direct campaigns yourself rather than running AdSense. | Confirm whether 731 is open without extra licence. |
| 6202 Computer consultancy | Only if you start selling engineering services. | Not core. |

**Do not apply for telecommunications (61) or linear broadcasting (60) activities.** Those are different businesses (TRA / broadcast). Whisco is an AVOD/FAST *aggregator and licensed-VOD player*, not a terrestrial or satellite broadcaster.

## 2.3 Electronic-media law flag (read this)

Law No. 41 of 2025 amending the Press, Printing and Publishing Law created a licensing regime for “electronic media sites” (electronic publishing houses, electronic newspapers, and other sites the Minister may designate). The statute is aimed at news/media sites, not every website in the Kingdom. A free streaming aggregator that hosts programmes could, depending on ministerial implementing decisions, be read as in-scope.

**Action:** on the same week you file the WLL, have counsel send a one-page query to the Ministry of Information / Publication and Press Directorate: *“AVOD/FAST platform aggregating official FTA streams and licensed VOD; no newsroom; no user-generated news. Do we need an electronic-media-site licence in addition to CR activities 6312/6201/6311?”* Get the answer in writing. Do not guess.

Until that answer exists, do not market Whisco as a “TV station” or “broadcaster” on official filings.

# 3. Step-by-step Sijilat walkthrough (single-shareholder WLL)

Portal: **https://www.sijilat.bh**  
You need: **eKey** (advanced) tied to the founder’s CPR.

## Step 0 — Documents to gather (Day 0)

- CPR and passport bio page (clear colour scans).
- Personal mobile number and email you will still own in five years (this becomes the company’s official contact).
- Three name choices in English and Arabic.
- Proposed Arabic trade name. Suggestion: **وسكو ميديا ذ.م.م.** / **وسكو تي في** as the brand.
- Proof of registered address: virtual-office lease **or** a family-owned property title + municipality letter. Sign the virtual-office contract only after name reservation is accepted, but line the provider up now.
- Short company profile (use the 2-pager in this pack).
- Source-of-funds note for the bank later: “personal savings + informal family/angel stipend of USD 200/month; platform pre-revenue.”
- Decide declared capital: **BHD 1,000** is the practical sweet spot — high enough that a bank does not laugh, low enough that BCCI stays in the BHD 16 band, and it is working capital not a sunk fee.

## Step 1 — eKey and Sijilat login

1. If you do not have advanced eKey, get it via the national eKey service before anything else.
2. Log into sijilat.bh.
3. Confirm your personal profile (CPR, nationality, address) is current.

## Step 2 — Reserve the commercial name

1. Public Services / New CR / Name reservation.
2. Submit three names in preference order. Avoid names of existing CRs, royal names, and regulated words (“bank”, “insurance”, “university”, “Bahrain TV”).
3. Pay the reservation fee.
4. Wait for approval (often same day to 2 working days).
5. Print/save the name-acceptance notice. It expires; file the CR before it lapses.

## Step 3 — Apply for a new Commercial Registration

1. Choose legal form: **With Limited Liability Company (W.L.L.)**.
2. Shareholders: **1**. Shareholder type: individual. Nationality: Bahraini. Shareholding: 100%. Capital: BHD 1,000 divided into 1,000 shares of BHD 1 (or 100 shares of BHD 10 — either is fine).
3. Appoint the founder as **Authorized Manager / Director** with full signing authority.
4. Registered office: paste the virtual-office address exactly as the provider writes it (block, road, building, governorate).
5. Financial year: 1 January – 31 December.
6. Add activities **6312, 6201, 6311** (confirm exact labels in the activity search; wording on Sijilat is what matters).
7. For 6312, enter **https://whisco.tv** in the website field.
8. UBO declaration: the founder is the sole UBO at 100%.
9. Upload CPR, passport, name reservation, draft Declaration of Incorporation / MoA, address proof.
10. Submit. Pay CR fee (BHD 50) when prompted.

Bahraini-owned, unregulated activities: MOIC review is often **minutes to 3 working days**. Foreign or MIA-touched activities take longer.

## Step 4 — Notarise the constitutional document

For a single-shareholder WLL the constitutional instrument is typically a **Declaration of Incorporation** (sometimes still bundled with a short Memorandum).

1. Take the Sijilat-generated bilingual draft to a **Ministry of Justice public notary** (cheaper) or a licensed private notary.
2. Founder signs in person with CPR.
3. Pay ~BHD 30 public / more private.
4. Scan both sides. Upload the notarised PDF back to the Sijilat application.

Use the English template in §6.2 as a starting point. The notary will insist on their bilingual form — that is normal. Do not fight the official template; make sure the **commercial objects, capital, manager powers, and shareholding** match what you filed.

## Step 5 — Address, municipality, licence

1. Virtual-office provider issues a tenancy / registered-address certificate in the **exact approved company name**.
2. Upload to Sijilat.
3. Municipality pre-approval (BHD 10) — virtual offices in permitted buildings usually clear.
4. Pay activity fee (BHD 100) + licence fee (BHD 20).
5. Sijilat issues:
   - CR certificate (electronic).
   - Activity licence (1 year).
6. Download PDFs. Print two colour copies. Order a company stamp (optional but banks like it).

## Step 6 — BCCI membership

Via Sijilat → Bahrain Chamber registration/renewal. Capital BHD 1,000 → fee in the **BHD 16** band. Collect or download the membership card. Banks ask for it.

## Step 7 — NBR tax account (not VAT)

Open a National Bureau for Revenue profile against the new CR. You are **not** VAT-registered. You still want an NBR login so that when (if) turnover crosses BHD 18,750 / 37,500 you can file in days, not weeks.

## Step 8 — Corporate bank account (see §5)

Do this before you sign Filmhub. Counterparties will ask for an IBAN.

## Step 9 — Post-incorporation housekeeping (same week)

- Assign IP from founder to company (template §6.3). Date it the CR-issuance date.
- Put the CR number in the website footer and on app-store legal pages:  
  `Whisco TV is operated by Whisco Media W.L.L., CR [number], Kingdom of Bahrain.`
- Update AdSense payee **only after** the corporate account exists and Google support confirms how to migrate a personal publisher account — do not kill a mid-review account by editing the payee mid-flight. Sequence: AdSense approval on the current account → first payment method → then ask Google to transfer / dual-account.
- Apple Developer / Google Play: after store approvals, migrate the developer account to the organization, or create an organization account and transfer the apps. Do not interrupt the current iOS review or the Android 14-day closed-test clock.
- legal@whisco.tv remains the rights-holder contact.
- Calendar: CR expiry minus 30 days; BCCI renewal; virtual-office renewal; Filmhub reporting date.

## Step 10 — What you do *not* do on Day 1

- Open an LMRA employer file. No staff = no file = no monthly LMRA.
- Register for VAT.
- Add activity 591 “just in case”.
- Appoint a second shareholder “for optics”. You do not need one.
- Sign the Filmhub agreement in the founder’s personal name “to save time”.

# 4. Compliance doctrines that travel with the CR

These are operating rules, not slogans.

1. **The CR must describe what you actually do.** Web portal + software + hosting. If the catalogue later includes company-produced originals, revisit activity 591 with counsel.
2. **Anti-piracy is now a corporate representation.** Filmhub, Apple, and Google will treat a piracy incident as a company event. Keep the automated legality/geo pipeline; keep legal@whisco.tv monitored.
3. **One ad per page, never against the player, no pop-unders.** Also a licensing and brand issue.
4. **UBO stays accurate.** If the angel ever converts into equity, update Sijilat UBO within the statutory window.
5. **No fake traffic, no purchased MAU.** Bank and AdSense KYC will ask how users arrive. The answer is SEO, app stores, WhatsApp, human community posts.

# 5. Banking and payments map

## 5.1 What the money actually looks like

| Flow | Currency in | Currency you want | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense (Bahrain payee) | USD wire *(EFT is not on Google’s Bahrain list; cheque or wire are)* | BHD or USD | Corporate USD + BHD accounts |
| Apple App Store ads / IAP (if any) | USD via bank wire to the developer-account country | USD | Same corporate USD |
| Google Play | USD wire | USD | Same |
| Filmhub rev-share | USD, typically ACH/wire from the US | USD | Corporate USD; they will ask for W-8BEN-E |
| Angel stipend USD 200/mo | USD or BHD | BHD | Documented gift into corporate or founder-then-contribute |
| Infra (Vercel, Neon, Apple fee) | USD on cards | USD | Corporate card once the bank issues one |

BHD is pegged at **USD 1 = BHD 0.376** (BHD 1 ≈ USD 2.65). Hold USD receipts in USD; convert to BHD only for local bills. Do not invent a “hedge”.

**Stripe:** Bahrain is not a full Stripe domestic-acquire market. Stripe documents Bahrain for *cross-border payouts* (Connect) with IBAN + SWIFT, not as a place you easily open a Stripe account and charge GCC cards. Do not build a product plan that assumes Stripe Checkout in Bahrain. AdSense + (later) an approved ad server + bank wire is the stack. If you ever need to collect sponsorship invoices, issue a Bahraini tax invoice and take a bank transfer.

## 5.2 Banks that are realistic for a tiny Bahraini WLL

Ranked for a pre-revenue, founder-operated internet company — not for a trading house.

| Bank | Why consider | Friction | Practical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bahrain Islamic Bank (BisB)** | Has pushed digital onboarding (CR number + Wathiq). Local, NBB-owned. | Sharia review of “advertising / entertainment” is usually fine; gambling/ribawi content is not your catalogue. | Try the app first. Minimum operating balance often ~BHD 500. |
| **National Bank of Bahrain (NBB)** | Oldest local bank. Multi-currency, Fawri/Fawri+, USD clearing. DigiCorp exists. | Relationship-manager model. Will want a 2-page profile (you have one) and source-of-funds. | Best long-term “serious company” account if BisB is slow. |
| **BBK (Bank of Bahrain and Kuwait)** | Strong GCC rails. Multi-currency including USD. | Similar KYC to NBB. | Good backup if NBB queues. |
| **Al Salam Bank** | Digital-leaning Islamic. | Sharia + KYC. | Alternative if BisB declines. |
| **ila Bank** (AHLI United digital) | Built for individuals and light business. | May cap corporate features. | Useful as a **personal** holding account, not the Filmhub account. |
| HSBC / Standard Chartered | Global wires look pretty on a Filmhub form. | They want substance, forecasts, and often balances this business does not have. | Do not waste a week. Revisit at real revenue. |

**Suggested sequence:** (1) BisB digital application the day the CR PDF lands. (2) If silent for 10 days, walk into an NBB branch with the pack below. (3) Keep BBK as third.

## 5.4 AdSense specifically

Google’s published EMEA table lists **Bahrain: Cheque yes, EFT no, Wire yes, Hyperwallet no.** Plan on **USD wire** into the corporate USD account once the publisher account is a company payee.

Do not change the AdSense country or payee name while the mid-September re-review is open. After approval:

1. Receive the PIN / address verification at the registered address (virtual offices must actually accept Google’s postcard).
2. Add the corporate USD account as wire payee when earnings near USD 100.
3. Confirm with the bank that they credit incoming USD wires from Google/Citibank without blocking “advertising” as a category.

Apple and Google Play payouts follow the same IBAN + SWIFT pattern. Developer legal entity should eventually match the CR. Migrate **after** the current iOS review and Android closed-test finish.

## 5.5 W-8BEN-E for Filmhub

US marketplaces will request IRS Form W-8BEN-E from the WLL (not W-8BEN, which is for individuals). That form claims foreign status and, where relevant, treaty benefits. Bahrain–US treaty treatment of advertising/royalty income is a counsel question; default is: fill the form as a Bahraini WLL, Chapter 3 / 4 status “disregarded”? No — a WLL with legal personality is a corporation for this purpose unless counsel says otherwise. **Have the accountant tick the boxes. Do not improvise FATCA.**

## 6.4 Investor side-letter — current USD 200/month arrangement

### How to think about the money (read before choosing a form)

The existing arrangement is **USD 200 per month, informal, non-equity, goodwill**, plus a one-time USD 300 content/infra budget. That is how both sides have treated it. Documenting it does not require turning it into a financing.

Three honest options:

| | A — Gift / sponsorship letter *(recommended now)* | B — Convertible loan / note | C — Stay handshake |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | The supporter pays USD 200/month as a **gift or sponsorship**. No shares. No repayment. No cap table. | The supporter lends money (monthly advances accrue). At a later qualifying round the balance converts into shares at a discount or cap. | What you have today. |
| When it is fair | The supporter is helping a founder they know and does **not** want ownership. Matches the stated facts. | The supporter wants optionality on equity if Whisco ever raises. Both sides must want that. | Only as a stop-gap until A is signed. |
| Investor-readiness | Clean. A later priced round starts at 100% founder ownership. | Clean if the note is short and Bahrain-law. Messy if you copy a US SAFE and hope. | A real investor will ask “who is this person and what did they buy?” |
| Risk | Supporter can stop anytime (state a 30-day notice). | If you never raise, the loan may still be repayable on maturity unless you write it off as convertible-only. | Ambiguity. |

**Recommendation:** sign **Form A** this month. Park **Form B** as an exhibit you can use *if and only if* the supporter asks for equity optionality. Do not convert a goodwill stipend into a debt you cannot repay.

Bahrain has no statutory “SAFE”. A US-style SAFE is an odd instrument in front of a Bahraini notary and a future local investor. If you ever need conversion mechanics, use a **short convertible loan** under Bahrain law, not a YC SAFE pasted into Word.
