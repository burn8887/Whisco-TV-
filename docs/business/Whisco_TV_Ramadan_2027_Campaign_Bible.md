## 0. Doctrines that override every line in this file

1. We do not produce a Ramadan musalsal. We programme rooms from the catalog we already have.
2. No piracy language. Ever. Anti-pirate is the brand. No “Ramadan IPTV pack,” no sports-rights collage, no “5000 channels.”
3. No invented MAU, iftar reach, or “30 million expats watching.” Early-stage. Mark every target as a *projection*.
4. Community posts are human-only. Automation drafts; a person publishes. WhatsApp is not an API blast.
5. Max one ad per page, never next to the player. Patron credits live in chrome, ≥24 CSS pixels from any player edge.
6. Apps collect zero data until v1.1. No in-app SDK, no patronage tag in the v1 build.
7. No subscriptions. No “Ramadan VIP.” Free forever.
8. We do not publish an iftar timetable as a religious authority. Link a known source. Do not invent Maghrib.
9. Creative: no food-porn iftar that reads as a restaurant ad. No “watch instead of praying.” No party-Ramadan. The house is full; the stream should just work.
10. Executable by ≤2 people. If a plan needs a community manager, a media buyer and a designer, it is the wrong plan.
11. Patronage funds the room. It does not buy the programme, the catalogue order, or a claim we have not earned. One named patron per surface per period.
12. Capacity in any 30-day window still holds in Ramadan: **4 language-hub patrons, 2 calendar collections, 1 homepage underwrite, 4 WhatsApp patron mentions, 4 weekly-drop weeks.** Daily editorial WhatsApp is allowed; paid credits are not daily.

Same charter prices as `Whisco_TV_Patronage_Program.md`. Same credit anatomy. Ramadan is a *calendar*, not a second rate card.

### 5.1 Credit anatomy (never change)

1. Preamble — support verb.
2. Legal name.
3. Quiet line — ≤20 words EN, ≤20 words AR, value-neutral.
4. Mark — Patron / دعم.
5. Optional plain URL on collection pages and WhatsApp only.

Forbidden in the quiet line: best, leading, #1, cheapest, guaranteed, free, discount, limited time, download, sign up, you/your, act now, exclusive rates, any comparison.
