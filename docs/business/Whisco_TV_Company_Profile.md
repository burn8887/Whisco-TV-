Whisco TV is a free, advertising-supported (AVOD / FAST) streaming service built for the more than 30 million expatriates living in the six GCC states. It is not a subscription product, not a pirate IPTV reseller, and not an Arabic-premium studio. It is a legal, multi-language living-room service that collects what is already lawful to watch and makes it findable.

The name is the founder’s Shih Tzu. The tone of the product follows the dog: loyal, unpretentious, fast.

## Mission

Give every expat household in the Gulf a legal, free, premium-feeling way to watch television from home — and end the region’s dependence on pirate IPTV boxes.

## What the service is

- **Live TV:** 581 active free-to-air and official live channels, health-checked every six hours. Dead streams are hidden automatically and restored when they recover.
- **On demand:** 15,696 active titles across 13 languages, including 61 Turkish series (~3,650 episodes), 333 Arabic series (~7,100 episodes), 2,500+ official Hindi films, and substantial Malayalam, Telugu, Bangla, Filipino, Pakistani, and Indonesian shelves.
- **Surfaces:** web app at whisco.tv (live); Android on Google Play closed testing; iOS submitted to App Store review on 2 September 2026.
- **Access:** no account required. No subscription. Watchlist and resume live on-device.

## How content is sourced

Every title is legally sourced from official broadcaster channels and embeds, free-to-air transmissions, public domain, or — as licensed catalogues come online — contracts with rights holders. Before a title is shown it passes embeddability, duration and per-country geo checks for all six GCC states. Titles that fail geo checks are hidden, not shown broken. Sanctioned sources are blacklisted. Rights-holder notices go to legal@whisco.tv and are honoured.

Most current VOD plays inside official YouTube embeds. In those plays the originating broadcaster earns the in-player advertising. Whisco earns display advertising on the surrounding pages. Licensed files playing in Whisco’s own player are the path to standard video advertising.

Whisco does not buy pirated feeds, does not geo-unblock pay television, and does not sell subscriptions.

## Audience

Primary: Indian (including Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Bengali), Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Filipino, Indonesian, Nepali, Sri Lankan and Arab expatriate households in Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Oman, plus the large cross-community audience for Turkish drama. The product is built for multi-language homes, which is the normal GCC household, not the exception.

The service is early. Public launch of the apps is in progress. We do not publish traffic figures we have not earned.

## Commercial model

- Free forever. Advertising only. Never a paywall.
- Display advertising via Google AdSense (publisher account active; re-review in flight as of September 2026).
- Video pre-roll on own-player licensed titles, on a revenue-share basis with the licensor. No minimum guarantees, no upfront licence fees, no exclusivity sought at this stage.
- Direct sponsorships later, when there is audience evidence to sell. Not before.
- Advertising cap: one slot per page, never adjacent to the player, no pop-unders or redirect networks.

Infrastructure cost is under USD 50 per month (Next.js on Vercel, Neon Postgres, automated operations). The service is designed to be run by two people or fewer.

## What we want from a catalogue partner

A non-exclusive, revenue-share licence for GCC-clearable films and series that fit family living rooms — South Asian drama, family and faith, thriller, documentary. Delivery to our own player. Monthly reporting. No MG. No exclusivity. Start small (100–300 titles), measure, extend.
