Hostile read. The plan is more honest than most seed decks. That does not make the assumptions investable. It makes the contradictions easier to see.

What follows is a diligence attack on *assumptions*, not on the founder's character or the anti-piracy doctrine. Those are stated clearly. The model is not.

## Verdict in one paragraph

This is a two-week-old website with **no MAU, no revenue, a prior AdSense rejection, no company, no signed content deal, and an undocumented $200/month gift**. The document then produces a five-year three-scenario P&L to August 2031. The author correctly labels numbers `[EST]` and admits the first monthly table did not match the stated formula. The revised tables are **annual targets reverse-engineered into months**. That is a narrative calendar, not a model. A hostile reader should treat §1–§5 as a well-written option memo on whether a legal YouTube-wrapper plus a thin licensed shelf can become a small GCC publisher — and should not underwrite any MAU, RPM, Filmhub, or "$0 extra cash" line as if it were parameterized.

## Key attacks (compressed — full text in project chat log 2026-09-06)

**0. The document attacks itself:** FY5 revenue inconsistent between §1 ($20k/$80k/$248k) and §2.4 SOM sentence ($48k/$145k/$385k). Monthly P&L was rebuilt after formula failed — outputs chosen first. Founder labour $0 by axiom ("$0 external cash" true only if operator unpaid 5 years). Catalog depth (15,696 titles) used as traction while same memo admits most hours pay the broadcaster.

**1. Market size:** Ken/Mordor figures real but irrelevant — that pool is premium CTV with sales houses; a new Bahrain aggregator competes for remnant display. "Expat-relevant AVOD $250–450m" and "SAM $80–150m" have no allocation method. Headcount ≠ demand: large share of the 31–35m is shift-working shared-housing labour with a pirate box already on the TV.

**2. Audience assumptions break the P&L:** All revenue = MAU × sessions × pv × fill × rate; no left-hand variable is measured. Conservative FY1 implies ~45% compound monthly growth from standing start with no paid ads. Sessions 5–9/month is habit-app behaviour, not a Google-discovered catalog of other people's players. Retention 25/35/45% unmeasured; the product structurally teaches users to bookmark the official channel and leave.

**3. Unit economics circular:** RPM bands sourced from our own dossier, not third-party rate cards. New AdSense sites post-LVC-strike see weak fill and remnant pricing; South Asian-IP traffic in GCC is not UAE premium inventory. Ad doctrine (1 slot, never near player) is a structural RPM cap — "the 95% discount to Tubi is not a temporary haircut, it is the product." Fill ramps 20%→70% invented. Filmhub terms modeled as signed; they are not.

**4. Four binary gates treated as timing assumptions:** AdSense (already rejected once; re-review ≠ approval), Filmhub (phone call ≠ term sheet; catalog mix may be Western B-movies that don't attach to our demand core), iOS approval (IPTV-adjacent category risk), incorporation (formation cost optimistic once PDPL/counsel/licence questions included). Angel $200/mo modeled as 60-month certainty; it is a cancellable gift.

**5. Growth engines: four stories, zero measured yield.** SEO: days-old domain already punished for thinness, targeting SERPs occupied by incumbents and pirate blogs; title-page factory + IndexNow is how you get the next LVC strike. Stores: 26 testers is a process requirement, not demand. WhatsApp/communities: a map is not membership; rules correctly cap this engine below mix-table significance. Meta $110/mo buys a learning set, not MAU.

**6. Pirate-box switching is harder than modeled:** box already paid for; Whisco lacks EPL/cricket (the moments the box turns on); the reseller is a cousin; YouTube already is the legal free library; rights stay siloed. R6 (telco/Shahid copies wedge) scored complacently — default beats curation.

**7. Cash:** infra cost won't stay <$50 at scale (bandwidth is the under-priced line). Formation + licence + counsel realistically $5–15k, not $400–800. Real runway = "until the founder is tired."

**8. Year-5 options are not options:** lifestyle publisher at $80k rev with unpaid founder = a blog with a video tab; named acquirers do not buy YouTube wrappers.

## What it would not attack

Does not invent MAU/revenue · names the YouTube monetization hole · names pirate IPTV as incumbent · refuses subscriptions · scores platform/key-person risk at top · conservative FY1 in the hundreds is the right order of magnitude.

**Bottom line:** underwrite the doctrines, not the rates. Until AdSense, a CR, a signed no-MG licence in the right languages, and four weeks of real retention exist, the only non-fiction financial statement is: cash burn ~$25–50/month, funded by a cancellable gift, operator unpaid, revenue $0.
