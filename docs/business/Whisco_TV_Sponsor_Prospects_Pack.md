- Community posts are human-only. A sponsor mention on WhatsApp is a human message, not a blast bot.
- No subscriptions. We will not sell a “premium unskippable” package we do not have.
- Contracting entity is intended as **Whisco Media W.L.L.** (under formation). Until the CR exists we can take a pilot, invoice via a written side-letter, and re-paper after incorporation. We will not pretend we are already a licensed media house.
- Anything recommended here must be executable by ≤2 people.

# 2. Rate card (small-platform, GCC-realistic)

## 2.1 How this card is meant to be used

We are not a Metro billboard and we are not a Shahid home-page takeover. The comparable *decision* for a brand manager is:

- a nano/micro influencer post in the UAE (roughly $100–500 / AED 300–1,500), or
- a two-week Meta test (AED 3,000–5,000 spend is a common SME start), or
- a month of WhatsApp marketing for a small retailer (~AED 1,300 all-in at 5k messages).

So the card sits **under a committee threshold** and **above “please do it for exposure.”** All prices USD. Invoice in USD or BHD at the day’s official rate. VAT applied once we are registered.

**Two columns on purpose**

| Column | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Founding (now → first meaningful analytics)** | Audience is early. We sell the room, the catalogue, the honesty, and a price that is easy to write off as a test. Every founding buy includes a written 30-day review and a make-good (extra days or an extra WhatsApp) if delivery is thin. |
| **Standard (publish only after 60 days of first-party analytics)** | Replace the “TBC” cells in the media kit. Raise prices. Stop discounting except for 90-day prepaid. |

Do not show a brand the Standard column as if it is current inventory we are already delivering at scale. That is a lie.

**What we refuse to sell.** Pre-roll (not live on licensed files yet). Pause ads. Pop-outs. “Native” posts in Facebook groups. Fake follower packages. Exclusive site-wide sponsorship (we would be betting the company on one logo). Anything that needs an SDK in the v1 apps.

## 2.4 Commercial terms (short)

- **Minimum first PO:** $350 (one shelf) or the $550 starter test.
- **Payment:** 100% prepay on founding buys. 50/50 on standard 90-day once we have a track record.
- **Make-good:** if the 30-day note shows delivery below what we described as *available inventory that week* (pages live, shelf live, WhatsApp sent), we extend days or add a send. We do not refund in cash on founding rates; we said the audience was early.
- **Lead capture:** outbound links only, to a URL they own. We do not host forms that collect personal data on their behalf.
- **Creative:** they supply logo + 6-word line + destination URL + any legal line. We build. Two revisions.
- **Category exclusivity:** available only on festival bursts, and only on the named shelf, not the whole site.
- **Paper:** short insertion order (one page) + our ads policy. Until the CR exists, the IO names Ali Albaharna / Whisco TV and states the contract novates to Whisco Media W.L.L. on incorporation.
- **Kill:** either side, 7 days’ written notice after the first 14 days of a 30-day unit. Unused prepaid 90-day days convert to WhatsApp mentions, not cash.

## 2.5 How a month of inventory actually looks

Solo-founder constraint: we can run **four paying shelves**, **two branded collections**, **one homepage placement**, and **four WhatsApp mentions** in any 30-day window without the product looking rented. If a fifth brand arrives, they wait or they buy the next window. Scarcity is real; do not oversell it as a strategy.
