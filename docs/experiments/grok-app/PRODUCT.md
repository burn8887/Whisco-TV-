## Five largest GCC expatriate communities

Source: Al Jazeera, 9 March 2026, citing Global Media Insight; Times of India 25 March 2026; Times Kuwait 11 March 2026.

| Rank | Community | GCC population | What they actually watch |
|------|-----------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Indian | 9.1 million | Hindi cinema/serials, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi. FTA news. Cricket legally where it exists (not Hotstar). |
| 2 | Bangladeshi | 5.04 million | Bangla natok, Dhaka news, cricket. YouTube-native. |
| 3 | Pakistani | 4.9 million | Urdu dramas, Pakistani news, PSL. OSN/beIN are the costly legal path. |
| 4 | Egyptian | 3.3 million | Musalsalat, Egyptian cinema, Arabic-dubbed Turkish dizi, Ramadan. |
| 5 | Filipino | 2.2 million | Teleseryes, Filipino movies, Manila news. iWantTFC geo-blocked. |

India is not one language. Hubs are languages, not nationalities.

## Concept

Language-first, household-aware, live-leaning. Tonight / Live / Library / You.

Premium = structure, speed, craft. Not paywalls.

## Bold bets

1. Faces, not accounts
2. Home Time
3. The legal Jadoo (phone as remote)

## Monetization besides “show ads”

Hub sponsorship: one brand per language hub per week. Never on the player.

## Whisco

Icon, empty state, buffering companion. Dry voice. No “Woof.” Sound off by default.
