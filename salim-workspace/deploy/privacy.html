Privacy policy

Applies to the Salim Android and iOS applications. Effective 20 October 2026. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Draft for preview — not yet submitted to a store. The registered operating entity still has to be named, and the policy must be reviewed again before live regional data, accounts, subscriptions or reporting back-ends are enabled. This text describes the current demonstration build.

Who we are

Salim is operated by OPERATING ENTITY (“we”, “us”), the entity responsible for the app and this website. Privacy requests: legal@salimdrive.com.

What the app does

Salim provides road-awareness prompts — such as upcoming cameras, road hazards, speed bumps and average-speed sections — based on your device’s location, speed and direction of travel. The current demonstration build ships a fictional Bahrain data pack and has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, billing or connected live feed.

Location

A simulated drive inside the app needs no location permission. When you explicitly start a device GPS test or drive, we process precise coordinates, speed, heading, accuracy and sample timing in memory to match nearby alerts and calculate section averages. On Android this runs in a foreground service with a visible ongoing notification; on iOS it uses location updates, which may continue while the test is active in the background.

Ending the session in the app, or from its Android notification, stops location updates. You can also revoke permission in system settings. Operating-system suspension or force-quitting the app can interrupt awareness. In this build, precise routes are not stored and are not uploaded to a Salim server.

Stored on your device

The app stores your voice preference, the local alert pack, trip date/duration/alert count and test mode, and any community report drafts you type. Drafts are not transmitted or published. Trip summaries are capped at 200 and drafts at 100; older items are removed when the cap is exceeded. You can delete trip history in My trips and individual drafts in Community. Deleting the app removes its local app storage, subject to your device’s backup and restore behaviour. Android backup is disabled in this build; iOS device backups may include local records according to your settings.

System and third-party services

  • Maps. On iOS, Apple MapKit may request map tiles and related content from Apple. Offline alert matching does not require those tiles.
  • External navigation. If you open a destination in another maps app, the destination you entered is shared with that app and its provider.
  • Speech and notifications. Text-to-speech and notifications use operating-system services. An installed Android speech engine may use its own network services.

These providers’ own terms and privacy practices apply. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run advertising or tracking SDKs in this build.
  • We do not publish your community drafts.
  • We do not keep a server-side copy of your precise route.

Children

Salim is a driving-awareness tool intended for licensed drivers and their passengers. It is not directed at children.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke location permission at any time, end a session to stop processing, and delete local summaries and drafts inside the app. For access, correction or deletion requests relating to anything we hold, contact legal@salimdrive.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data-protection law; we will respond within the period required by that law.

Changes

If the app begins using live regional data, accounts, payments or a reporting back-end, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled, and the effective date above will change.

Not a safety or emergency service

Salim is an awareness aid. It does not guarantee that you will avoid a hazard, a fine or a collision, and it is not a substitute for attention, posted signs or traffic law. In an emergency in Bahrain, call 999.
