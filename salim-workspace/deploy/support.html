Support

Set everything up while safely parked. Never adjust the app while driving.

Where the data comes from. Camera positions come from a bundled Bahrain pack and live OpenStreetMap data, which volunteer mappers survey and which can be wrong, out of date or missing. Do not rely on an alert instead of the road. In an emergency in Bahrain, call 999.

Contact

Email support@salimdrive.com. Please include your device model, OS version, app version and, if possible, the road name and approximate time. Do not send screenshots containing your home address or other personal detail.

Data corrections: tell us the road, direction of travel, alert type and what you observed. Camera and hazard locations change; reported corrections are reviewed before any future data release.

Getting started

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and no account. The app is free to use, with an optional Salim Premium subscription that removes advertising and unlocks the wider offline camera pack. Camera and hazard warnings are never held back without it.
Can I try it without granting location?
Yes. Choose Run a simulated drive. The simulation is foreground-only and ends when you leave the app.
What does the device GPS test actually do?
It uses precise location, speed and heading to match nearby demo alerts and calculate section averages. It is started explicitly, may continue in the background until you end it, and shows an ongoing notification on Android.

Permissions and battery

Why does Salim need location “all the time” on iOS?
Only so an explicitly started session can keep working while the screen is locked or another app is in front. Ending the session stops updates.
Will it drain my battery?
Continuous location does use power. End sessions you are not using. Some devices apply aggressive battery optimisation that can interrupt background work; if awareness stops while locked, check your device’s battery settings for Salim.
Location permission was denied or set to approximate
Simulation still works. The device GPS test needs precise location; without it, Salim will not invent a position or an average.

Audio

Salim talks over my music or navigation
Prompts are short and should duck other audio briefly. If another app stays quiet after a prompt, end the session and reopen the app; report it with your device and OS version so we can reproduce it.
Can I mute speech?
Yes. Turn off Voice alerts in the app or in Settings.
