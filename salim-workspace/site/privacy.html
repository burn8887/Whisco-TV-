Privacy policy

Applies to the Salim Android and iOS applications. Effective 20 October 2026. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Who we are

Salim is operated by Ali Sameer Sadeq Mohamed Albaharna (“we”, “us”), the individual responsible for the app and this website. Privacy requests and support: legal@salimdrive.com and support@salimdrive.com.

What the app does

Salim gives road-awareness prompts — upcoming cameras, road hazards, roadworks, traffic and speed sections — based on your device’s location, speed and direction of travel. It is an awareness aid, not a navigation or emergency service.

Location

A simulated drive inside the app needs no location permission. When you start a device GPS test or drive, we process precise coordinates, speed, heading, accuracy and sample timing in memory to match nearby alerts. On Android this runs in a foreground service with a visible ongoing notification; on iOS it uses location updates, which may continue while a session is active in the background.

Ending the session, or the Android notification, stops location updates. You can revoke permission in system settings at any time. Precise routes are not stored and are not uploaded to a Salim server. Trip summaries kept on the device hold a start time, duration, alert count and whether the session was simulated — never a route.

Camera data from OpenStreetMap

To know where cameras are, the app asks the OpenStreetMap Overpass service for cameras in a rectangle around you. That request contains the rectangle, not your exact position, and no account, advertising identifier or device identifier. Results are cached on the device so the next drive does not repeat the request; at most one request is made per area every thirty minutes. Camera data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Mapped positions can be wrong, out of date or missing.

Maps

On Android the map is drawn from OpenStreetMap raster tiles, so tile requests reach OpenStreetMap’s tile servers and reveal the tiles you viewed and your IP address. On iOS the map is Apple MapKit, which requests map tiles and related content from Apple. Alert matching works without either.

Advertising

The free tier of the store build shows advertising served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device information and, where you have consented, advertising identifiers to select and measure ads. We do not pass your location, trip data or the camera feed to the ad SDK, and no advertisement is ever shown on the drive screen — the screen you see while a session is running stays clear. In regions where consent is required, a consent prompt appears before any ad loads, and you can change your choice later in the app’s settings.

Salim Premium removes advertising. Google’s privacy policy applies to the ad service: policies.google.com/privacy.

Subscriptions

Salim Premium is sold through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is processed by the store; we never see or store your card details. The app receives only whether a valid subscription exists, so it can unlock the paid features, and Play or Apple records the purchase against your store account as their terms describe.

No safety alert is ever withheld without a subscription. Camera and hazard warnings are free. Premium covers advertising removal and the wider offline camera pack.

Stored on your device

The app stores your voice and tone preferences, appearance, the local alert pack, cached camera data, trip summaries and any community report drafts you type. Drafts are not transmitted or published. Trip summaries are capped at 200 and drafts at 100. You can delete trip history in Trips and individual drafts in Community. Deleting the app removes its local storage, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Text to speech and notifications

Voice alerts use operating-system speech services. An installed Android speech engine may use its own network services under its own policy. Notifications are generated on the device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not keep a server-side copy of your route.
  • We do not publish your community drafts.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising described above.

Children

Salim is intended for licensed drivers and their passengers. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke location permission, end a session at any time, delete local summaries and drafts inside the app, and manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play or the App Store. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact legal@salimdrive.com. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under applicable data-protection law, and we will respond within the period that law requires.

Changes

If the app begins using accounts, a reporting back-end or additional third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled, and the date above will change.

Not a safety or emergency service

Salim does not guarantee that you will avoid a hazard, a fine or a collision, and it is not a substitute for attention, posted signs or traffic law. In an emergency in Bahrain, call 999.
