Know what’s ahead.
Keep your calm.

Salim is a calm co-pilot for the road ahead. It tells you what is coming — cameras, hazards, speed bumps and average-speed sections — for the direction you are actually travelling, without taking over the drive.

Where the data comes from. Camera positions ship in a bundled Bahrain pack and are fetched live from OpenStreetMap contributors for the area around you. Coverage is what volunteer mappers have surveyed — dense on Gulf motorways, patchy elsewhere — so a position can be wrong, out of date or missing. Salim is not a navigation or emergency service and guarantees no outcome on the road. Always follow posted signs and traffic rules.

What Salim does

  • Relevant. Alerts are filtered by your heading, so cameras on the opposite carriageway stay quiet.
  • Considerate. Short spoken prompts that coexist with navigation and music instead of competing with them.
  • Offline-ready. Alert data is stored on the device, so awareness does not depend on a live connection.
  • Section-aware. Average speed across section-control zones is calculated continuously, including time stopped.
  • Honest. Coverage, freshness and uncertainty are stated plainly. No claim of official endorsement.

Where Salim is going

Bahrain first, then the wider Gulf. Later work includes live regional data, car-display support, watch haptics and vehicle-aware automation. Those integrations are not in the current build, and they will not be described as available until they ship.
