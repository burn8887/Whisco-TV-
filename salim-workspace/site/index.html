Salim is a calm road-awareness companion. It can show nearby mapped cameras and road alerts for your direction of travel without taking over the drive. Coverage is incomplete and no verified GCC camera pack is bundled in this build.

Where the data comes from. This build has no verified static Bahrain or GCC camera pack. Nearby mapped camera records may be fetched from OpenStreetMap contributors; volunteer data can be wrong, out of date or missing. Community reporting is not connected to a live service. Salim is not a navigation or emergency service and cannot predict road outcomes. Always follow posted signs and traffic rules.

What Salim does

  • Relevant. Alerts are filtered by your heading, so cameras on the opposite carriageway stay quiet.
  • Considerate. Short spoken prompts that coexist with navigation and music instead of competing with them.
  • Locally cached. Nearby mapped data may remain available offline after a successful fetch; coverage outside the cached area may be unavailable.
  • In development. Verified regional camera datasets, community moderation and section-control coverage are not ready in this build.
  • Honest. Coverage, freshness and uncertainty are stated plainly. No claim of official endorsement.

Where Salim is going

Bahrain first, then the wider Gulf. Future work includes properly licensed verified regional data, live human moderation, car-display support, watch haptics and vehicle-aware automation. These are not available until their data, hosting and device tests are complete.
