## Executive position

The legal operator is **NOT IN DATA**. The Founder has not confirmed whether Salim will operate through an existing entity or a new Salim-specific entity. “Whisco Media W.L.L” appears in earlier project notes only as an unverified candidate; it is not established as Salim’s registered owner, operator, or contracting party. Do not put that name, the Founder’s personal name, or an assumed company number in applications, contracts, store disclosures, or public policies until documentary evidence and the Founder’s written approval are obtained.

A Bahrain W.L.L. is one possible form, not a recommendation. Legislative Decree No. 28 of 2020 amended Article 261 to say a W.L.L. may be owned by one natural or legal person. However, MOIC’s 2024 Procedures Guide describes W.L.L. as having 2–50 partners. The primary-law amendment and that guide conflict. Before choosing a single-member W.L.L. or filing any structure, obtain written confirmation of current Sijilat treatment and Bahrain counsel’s advice. Do not use an informal nominee or artificial second shareholder to work around the conflict.

## Recommended workflow (gated; not a filing checklist guarantee)

1. **Confirm the operating model.** Decide whether a documented existing entity will own and operate the app, or whether a new entity is intended. If using an existing entity, verify its current CR, legal form, ownership/UBO, activities, good standing, authority to operate the app, and permission to use its address and payment facilities.
2. **Resolve legal form and one-owner question.** Obtain current Sijilat guidance for the exact applicant, nationality, ownership, and activities. Ask how it applies Legislative Decree No. 28 of 2020 (Article 261) alongside the 2024 Procedures Guide’s 2–50 wording. Obtain written counsel advice. The 2025 amendment, Legislative Decree No. 38 of 2025, also changed the Commercial Companies Law; review the Arabic Official Gazette text and current implementing measures.
3. **Classify every activity before reserving/finalizing the application.** Ask MOIC/Sijilat to classify: mobile application operation, online services, subscriptions, advertising, user-generated/community reports, data processing, marketplace or transaction functions if any, and any future paid offerings. MOIC’s eCommerce guidance distinguishes online retail (activity 4791) from operating e-marketplaces/websites/web portals (activity 6312). Do not assume either code fits Salim; get a written classification and every required NOC/sector approval.
4. **Check name, ownership eligibility and documents.** Confirm English and Arabic trade names, permitted ownership and nationality conditions, manager eligibility, address, capital, memorandum/articles form, notarization or electronic execution path, translations/attestations, and any dependent approvals in Sijilat for the actual profile.
5. **Apply only after Founder sign-off.** The applicant or authorized representative files through the current Sijilat workflow, pays only after transaction-specific written approval, answers authority requests, and retains receipts and final stamped/registered documents. No agent should submit or pay on the Founder’s behalf without explicit authorization.
6. **After CR issuance, complete operational setup.** Verify the CR and all licensed activities; establish bank/payment facilities as applicable; register for tax/VAT when required; complete UBO/beneficial-owner filings and annual CR renewals; implement accounting, invoicing, consumer complaints/refunds, records retention, privacy governance, and required licenses before commencing the relevant activity.
7. **Reconcile public identity.** Only after the legal entity, CR, activities, contact channel and processing facts are confirmed should company name and contact details be inserted in store listings, privacy notices, terms, invoices, website, and app disclosures. Do not publish first and “correct later.”

## Official-source notes and material caveats

- **MOIC Procedures Guide (2024):** activity/legal-form selection and approvals are application-specific. Its 2–50 W.L.L. partner-count statement conflicts with the later 2020 amendment; treat the count as unresolved, not as current settled law. [MOIC guide](https://www.moic.gov.bh/sites/default/files/2024-03/Procedures%20Guide%20for%20Establishments%20and%20Commercial%20Companies.pdf)
- **Legislative Decree No. 28 of 2020:** the official LLOC English guidance translation of amended Article 261 says an LLC may be owned by one natural or legal person; LLOC warns Arabic Official Gazette text is authoritative. Verify the current consolidated Arabic law and Sijilat implementation before relying on the English translation. [LLOC amendment record](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/en/Legislation/Amendments/L2101) · [LLOC English translation](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/FullEn/L2820.docx)
- **Legislative Decree No. 38 of 2025:** LLOC’s official amendment record lists the decree in Official Gazette issue 3832 on 11 September 2025, with Arabic image/text available. It amended the Commercial Companies Law after the 2020 amendment. A secondary research overview reports changes concerning manager/director liability, electronic meetings and voting, single-shareholder closed joint-stock companies, and other matters; rely on the Arabic decree and counsel, not this summary, for legal conclusions. [LLOC amendment record](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/en/Legislation/Amendments/L2101) · [LLOC Arabic text](https://www.lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/L3825) · [Library of Congress overview](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2026-05-13/bahrain-updated-commercial-companies-law-takes-effect)
- **Activity classification:** MOIC’s eCommerce information distinguishes internet retail activity 4791 and eMarketplaces/websites/web portals activity 6312, including app subscription concepts in its FAQ. This is a prompt to seek a decision for Salim’s exact facts, not a code recommendation. [MOIC eCommerce](https://www.moic.gov.bh/en/node/5815) · [MOIC FAQ](https://www.moic.gov.bh/en/faq/27)
- **CR renewal:** Bahrain’s government service catalogue describes CR renewal as annual. Verify fees, dates, grace periods and entity-specific duties in the live Sijilat account. [Bahrain government service](https://www.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BNP/ServicesCatalogue/GSX-UI-PServiceDetails?psID=2036)
- **UBO:** MOIC provides UBO information for CR holders. Confirm the current threshold, exemptions, evidence, deadlines and changes for the chosen form. [MOIC UBO](https://www.moic.gov.bh/en/node/2778)
- **Bank/payment obligations:** MOIC describes Resolution No. 43 of 2024 and commercial bank/e-payment account requirements. Obtain an applicability determination for the selected entity, activity, and payment methods before stating a universal obligation. [MOIC announcement](https://www.moic.gov.bh/en/node/5732)
- **VAT:** NBR’s current registration page states resident mandatory registration at BHD 37,500 taxable supplies and optional registration at BHD 18,750. Ask a Bahrain tax adviser how app-store settlements, subscriptions, advertising, nonresident customers and cross-border sales are treated. Do not treat those thresholds as a complete tax analysis. [NBR VAT registration](https://www.nbr.gov.bh/vat_registration)
