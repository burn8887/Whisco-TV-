# Name clearance — “Salim” (working notes, not legal advice)

Status as of 20 October 2026. **No trademark register was queried.** No domain was purchased, no
store record created, no handle reserved. This file records only what was actually observed, so the
founder and counsel can see which risks are known and which are still open.

## 1. Why the name changed

The previous working name **Sahm (سهم)** was dropped. It means “arrow” but also “share”, collides
with a large Saudi trading app, and is ambiguous when pronounced in English. See the PR history.

## 2. Meaning and linguistic fit

**Salim / سليم** — from the root س-ل-م: safe, sound, whole, free of defect. Related to *salām*
(peace) and *salāma* (safety). Pronounced *sa-LEEM*. It is a very common male given name across the
Arab world, and also exists as *Saleem* (same word, different transliteration) and *Salem/Sālim*
(سالم, a related but distinct name). **Keep one Latin spelling: Salim.**

- Strength: instantly understood in Bahrain and the GCC; no finance or enforcement connotation.
- Weakness: a common personal name and a laudatory word for a safety product. Distinctiveness must
  come from the mark, the signature and the store descriptor, not the word alone.

require unique listing titles, so a bare “Salim” title is unlikely to be accepted or discoverable.
Use a descriptor in the title (see §5).

## 5. Practical recommendations

1. **Store titles**
   - Google Play: `Salim — Road Awareness` (30-character limit applies; verify in Play Console).
   - App Store: `Salim: Road Awareness` with subtitle `Know what's ahead.`
2. **File the logo and a compound mark**, not only the word. A word mark for a laudatory Arabic
   adjective in Class 9 (software), 39 (navigation/transport) and 42 (SaaS) is likely to attract
   descriptiveness objections and prior-use citations.
3. **Clear the exact list before filing:** Bahrain Ministry of Industry and Commerce trademark
   search, Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP), UAE Ministry of Economy, and — if
   distributing there — EUIPO/USPTO. Use a local agent; this note is not a search report.
4. **Check store search in Arabic** for `سليم` in Bahrain, Saudi Arabia and the UAE on the actual
   devices, in the actual storefronts. Screenshot the results as evidence.
5. **Avoid official-looking cues.** No police shield, no radar dish, no “official”, no government
   colours. Salim must never look like a traffic authority.
6. **Keep the domain registration and the trademark filing in the same name** wherever possible.
   Mismatched ownership is a recurring problem in later enforcement and store disputes.
7. **Protect the brand in use.** Consistent mark, consistent spelling, `™` once a filing exists, `®`
   only after registration. Keep dated evidence of first use in commerce.
8. **Re-check after launch.** Same-name apps appear often; set a quarterly store and register check.
