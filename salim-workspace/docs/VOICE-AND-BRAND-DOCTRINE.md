# Salim Voice & Brand Doctrine

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Owner:** Founder; product language can be localized only after native-speaker review

## 1. Brand hierarchy

| Voice | Where it appears | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Company voice** | Legal pages, support, press, partner and store correspondence | Plain, factual, accountable; do not make claims before entity, data, and service evidence exists |
| **Product voice** | Onboarding, dashboard, settings, alert copy, help | Calm, brief, specific, respectful; speak as a competent passenger, then stop |
| **Alert voice** | Audio and active-drive display | One useful fact and distance/direction qualifier; no joke, sales copy, urgency inflation, or repeated chatter |
| **Moderator/support voice** | Report review and user correction response (future) | Explain receipt/review/outcome without exposing reporter identity or promising acceptance |
| **Mascot/character** | None approved | No mascot/persona may be invented; “Salim” is a product name, not a fictional person claiming authority |

## 2. Tone rules

- Calm rather than alarmist; adult rather than cute; precise rather than bureaucratic.
- State what is known, how approximate it is, and what to do safely (e.g. follow posted signs), not what outcome is guaranteed.
- Prefer “camera mapped ahead” over “camera detected.” The app reads map data; it does not sense enforcement equipment.
- Prefer “coverage varies; some cameras may be missing or outdated” over “verified GCC coverage.”
- Never shame a driver, celebrate high speed, coach evasion, or imply a ticket is avoided.
- Do not address the user while driving with questions that require a response.

## 3. Claim-frequency and evidence caps

1. **Coverage caveat:** show at least one clear caveat in each distinct data surface (map/list/onboarding) and a concise version in each store listing. Do not repeat it on every spoken warning if the active drive screen retains a visible coverage status.
2. **“Live”:** use only when the underlying request has just succeeded and name the source (e.g. “OSM query updated”); never use it as a general product adjective. Current feed is a query against community-mapped data, not official live surveillance.
3. **“Verified”:** reserve for a documented human-reviewed record and describe the review scope. A moderator action or community vote does not verify a user’s identity, physical presence, or future accuracy.
4. **Counts, rates, percentages, user numbers, savings, safety outcomes:** do not publish unless supported by a dated reproducible record. Current numbers are NOT IN DATA; actual users/revenue are 0 until measured.
5. **“Free trial”:** use only when the relevant store offer exists and that user is eligible. Otherwise say the subscription price and renewal terms as required by the store.
6. **Official/complete/accurate:** prohibited unless an official source, licence, date, exact territory, and a maintenance process support the precise claim.

## 4. Approved core copy (subject to operational truth)

- Brand line: **Know what’s ahead. Keep your calm.**
- Positioning: **The calm co-pilot for the road ahead.**
- Product descriptor: **Road awareness assistant.**
- Safety caveat: **Camera coverage varies. Mapped positions may be incomplete or out of date. Follow posted signs and traffic rules.**
- Alert pattern: **[hazard] · [direction/distance] · [uncertainty qualifier if applicable]**. Example: “Mapped camera ahead, your direction. About 300 metres.”
- Offline/source limitation: state that cached data may remain available, but do not imply every area has current offline camera data.
- Reporting copy (only after the service/UI is deployed): “Your report is a suggestion for human review, not a verified camera or proof of identity/presence. Do not submit while driving.”

## 5. Localization policy

### English

Use clear international English. Avoid enforcement slang, guarantees, or legal conclusions. Keep spelling consistent in UI and store listing. Distinguish fixed mapped cameras from mobile/patrol units.

### Arabic

- Product name is exactly **سالم**; never سليم. Latin product name remains **Salim**.
- MSA tagline draft: **اعرف ما أمامك. قُد بهدوء.** It is not approved as final until a native Bahraini/Gulf reviewer reads it in context and checks diacritics, RTL punctuation, screen reader order, and colloquial interpretation.
- Gulf colloquial draft is optional marketing copy only; do not insert into safety instructions without local review.
- Any Arabic safety, legal, purchase, privacy, consent, or location disclosure requires professional translation and local legal review before release.

### Other GCC languages

No language beyond English/Arabic is currently evidenced as supported. Do not claim Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam, or other localization until the full critical path (permissions, safety alerts, privacy, billing, support) is translated and tested by native reviewers.

## 6. Brand usage

- Use **Salim — Road Awareness** in store titles and **Salim** in running text.
- Use the road-S mark on approved assets only; do not alter it or pair it with police lights, radar dishes, sirens, speed trophies, or enforcement imagery.
- Never call the product a radar detector, police detector, ticket blocker, navigation app, official service, or emergency tool.
- Do not use founder name, company name, “Bahrain Government,” or authority logos as an endorsement without documentary permission.
- No “Whisco” product association in customer-facing Salim copy unless the Founder confirms the legal/corporate relationship and disclosure is appropriate.

## 7. Review checklist for new copy

- [ ] Is every factual statement sourced and current as of a date?
- [ ] Does the wording distinguish map data from physical detection?
- [ ] Does the caveat explain that coverage can be incomplete/outdated?
- [ ] Could the copy encourage unsafe driving, distraction, evasion, or trust in a wrong coordinate?
- [ ] Is the language fully localized and accessibility-checked for the target market?
- [ ] Is company/legal identity verified before use?
- [ ] Is an offer (price/trial/feature) actually configured in the store and available to this user?

If any answer is uncertain, hold the copy for Founder review; do not let an agent invent authority or certainty.
