## 1. Executive decision

Build and validate a Bahrain-first native road-awareness product, but do not yet fund a scale plan or publish revenue claims. The commercial thesis depends on three unproven conditions: (1) drivers value Salim's experience over free substitutes, (2) Salim can obtain and maintain sufficiently accurate, legally usable camera records, and (3) measured subscriptions and/or context-appropriate advertising cover the real cost to serve. None is established by the available evidence.

The product is not a general-purpose navigation replacement. Its central promise must remain modest: provide useful, sourced road-awareness information without guaranteeing that every camera or hazard is present. Fixed cameras only; no police/patrol/covert/radar-emission detection. Two fully native Apple/Android apps; Bahrain primary, GCC expansion gated per country. The approved product, brand and data boundaries are in the Product Blueprint, Voice & Brand Doctrine, Content/Inventory Doctrine, and Operating Rules.

**Decision:** continue low-cost validation and evidence work within existing approved authority. No new paid commitment, price publication, public launch, paid acquisition, data purchase, or release is authorized by this plan.

## 3. Customer value proposition (hypothesis, not public claim)

**Hypothesis:** Bahrain drivers who want calm, useful awareness of fixed cameras and road conditions may value a native experience that is explicit about its data sources, freshness and limits, and that avoids unnecessary interaction while driving.

What must be demonstrated before turning this into a claim:

- User interviews identify a specific unmet job not already adequately met by Waze, Radarbot, Bahrain MyGov, signs, or the driver's usual workflow.
- A lawful source and human/automated quality process provide sufficiently accurate, fresh data for a clearly stated country/area; no guessed or unlicensed records.
- Non-driving usability tests and a supervised beta show that alert design is understandable, non-intrusive and useful; safety outcomes are not inferred from usability alone.
- Actual repeated use demonstrates retention; price tests and real payments demonstrate willingness to pay.

## 5. Go-to-market sequence

1. **Truth and readiness:** reconcile public claims with source code and content inventory; remove any unsupported bundled-camera/coverage claim. Verify brand/domain/contact, app declarations, product IDs and account status.
2. **Evidence and rights:** close G1/G5 as relevant; secure country-specific provenance/permission/update process, then validate records. No expansion via a map toggle alone.
3. **Product validation:** conduct non-driving interviews and usability sessions; run a limited beta only after G2 and privacy/security review; measure correction rates, false positives, alert usefulness, repeat use, crashes and support burden.
4. **Production decision:** G3 plus separate Founder release authorization. No public promise that the service is complete, guaranteed or live across all GCC.
5. **Monetization test:** after the useful baseline, test approved price/concept and ad placement only with legal/privacy, store policy, consent and measurement readiness. All paid spend requires a stop rule and Founder approval.
6. **Scale:** only after G4, reconciled contribution margin, positive cohort evidence, support capacity and a current country data-rights basis; expansion remains country-by-country.

### Transparent formulas (populate only with actual data)

- `Subscription gross receipts = paid subscribers × realized local price × paid periods`.
- `Subscription net receipts = gross receipts − refunds/chargebacks − applicable store fees − applicable indirect taxes/withholding`.
- `Ad gross revenue = eligible measured impressions ÷ 1,000 × realized eCPM`; report by country, placement, consent status and period. Do not use advertised network averages as Salim actuals.
- `Contribution = net receipts − hosting/API − data licence/validation − moderation/support − privacy/security/compliance − attributable acquisition − other variable costs`.
- `Monthly break-even paid equivalents = (monthly fixed cost − measured net ad contribution) ÷ measured net contribution per paid subscriber`. If denominator is zero/negative or missing, break-even is **NOT IN DATA**.
- `Cash runway = available business cash ÷ verified monthly cash burn`; available cash and monthly burn are **NOT IN DATA**, so runway is **NOT IN DATA**.

## 9. Measurement dashboard and decision cadence

Monthly after a properly authorized beta/release: installs by storefront/country; activated users; weekly/monthly active users; cohort retention; completed trips (only privacy-approved aggregate); alert impressions/interaction; false/missed/stale reports; corrections and time-to-resolution; crash-free sessions; support contacts; paid trial/paid conversion if lawful; refunds/renewals; reconciled store proceeds; ad impressions/fill/eCPM/consent; costs by fixed/variable line; contribution margin and cash balance. Every metric has source, geography, denominator and period. If access is missing, write `NOT IN DATA`.

Decision rules:

- Stop if a privacy/safety incident, invalid data-rights basis, severe false-alert cluster, or unmanageable moderation queue occurs; follow incident and recovery documents.
- Do not scale if the measured cohort's contribution after variable costs is negative or not measurable.
- Do not expand a country until that country's G5-equivalent rights/quality, legal, language and support review is signed off.
- Revisit pricing only with evidence from actual users and current fee/tax inputs; no permanent price inferred from an early survey.
