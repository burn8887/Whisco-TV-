# Salim Operating Rules

**Version:** 1.2
**Effective snapshot:** 2026-10-28
**Owner:** Founder; agents execute only within explicit authority
**Status:** Interim rules pending founder confirmation of legal entity and spending caps

## 1. Evidence and measurement rules

- Every factual metric must carry a snapshot date, time interval, unit, geography, source, and method. Preserve the source or a durable link/reference.
- Separate **measured actuals**, **founder-reported inputs**, **externally sourced facts**, and **estimates**. Never convert one class into another by paraphrase.
- Estimates must be labeled `[EST]`, show their inputs and calculation, and state what would invalidate them. No invented market counts, coverage counts, conversion rates, salaries, vendor prices, or growth rates.
- Missing information is written **NOT IN DATA**. A missing amount is not zero; actual revenue is shown as **$0 until measured** only where no receipt is evidenced.
- Maintain base currency as **BHD for Bahrain operations** when a source supports it; record original currency and conversion date for foreign prices. FX policy/rate source is **NOT IN DATA** pending finance setup.
- Public statements may not outpace evidence. Never claim all GCC cameras, official/complete coverage, guaranteed alerts, “verified users,” deployed reporting, or a free trial unless independently verified.
- External-reader financial pages omit an optimistic scenario. Show conservative and realistic bands only; if inputs do not support a band, mark **NOT IN DATA** rather than manufacture one.
- No TAM/SAM/SOM headline unless every denominator is dated, geographically scoped, source-linked, and definition-compatible. Vehicle registrations are not unique drivers or reachable app users; do not sum non-harmonized country figures.
- No “all GCC,” “complete,” “official,” or “verified coverage” claim without per-country written rights, a stated coverage method, field-quality evidence, and a dated freshness statement. A G5 pass for one source/country does not open another country.
- Do not describe community reports as shared, live, moderated, verified, or visible to other users until the production path, human moderator, privacy/security controls, and end-to-end evidence are verified. Local drafts must be labelled as such.
- A usability result does not prove crash reduction or safer driving. Do not claim a safety outcome without appropriate independent evidence and review; do not encourage speeding or enforcement evasion.

## 2. Binary gates

A gate is closed until the stated documentary proof is stored in the project library (no credentials or private customer data) and the Founder records a dated decision. A gate does not open by elapsed time or verbal optimism.

| Gate | Opens only when | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| **G1 — Entity authority** | The exact operating entity, active registration, permitted activity, ownership/signatory authority, and bank/payment recipient are verified for the intended operation | Official registration extract/licence and founder approval; redact personal identifiers before repository storage |
| **G2 — Beta distribution** | A current signed Salim build is accepted on the intended store test track; permanent app ID is verified; privacy, data-safety, location, ads, and app-access declarations match the build | Store-console release record, signed artifact checksum, CI run, completed review declarations; no credentials |
| **G3 — Production eligibility** | Store production access/review is granted and the Founder separately approves any public release | Console decision and explicit release authorization |
| **G4 — First verified receipt** | First real customer or store settlement reaches the designated business account and reconciles to the ledger after fees/refunds | Redacted transaction/settlement evidence and reconciliation entry |
| **G5 — Camera-data rights** | At least one relevant data source has written rights and a documented quality/update process acceptable for the target country; scope is stated per country/source | Signed licence/official terms, provenance, validation record, review sign-off |

**Current gate state as of 2026-10-28:** G1 **NOT VERIFIED**; G2 **NOT OPEN** (CI passed but no current signed store test upload evidenced); G3 **NOT OPEN**; G4 **NOT OPEN** (revenue $0 until measured); G5 **NOT OPEN** (no verified, redistribution-cleared static GCC dataset). The source-only report service does not open any gate.

## 3. Revenue and projection rule

Every revenue stream remains `$0 actual` until Gate G4 has qualifying payment evidence. Forecasts may be shown only as `[EST]` conservative and realistic scenarios with method and sensitivity; optimistic case is prohibited. Sales forecasts do not count as cash, and app-store gross receipts are not net revenue. Refunds, taxes, store fees, advertising fill, and settlement timing must be separately evidenced before unit economics are claimed.

Do not publish numeric multi-year revenue, break-even, or budget-to-income claims while price, users, fees, taxes, refunds, variable costs, or the Founder’s income target are missing. In that case publish the conservative/realistic formulas and `NOT IN DATA`; do not invent point estimates. Interview intent is not a purchase, competitor pricing is not Salim pricing, and an ad dashboard estimate is not settled cash.

The Billing Register records evidence; it does not authorize a purchase, renewal, vendor, campaign, paid study, or dataset commitment. Each requires a Founder-set cap and written, transaction-specific approval. Preserve original invoices privately and record a safe reference only in Git.

This is a planning/budget availability input; whether it is a hard cap, actual liquidity, or rollover budget is NOT IN DATA. Year 2–3 budgets, one-off and emergency caps, and the recurring transaction approval limit remain NOT IN DATA. This does not authorize any specific commitment. There is no implied authority from this table.

| Tier | Availability | Allowed spend (only after written Founder approval) | Never implied |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T0 — Preserve** | Always | No-cost maintenance and existing paid obligations whose cancellation would create a verified loss or safety/security risk | No new recurring service, ads, vendor, or commitment |
| **T1 — Validate identity** | After G1 | Necessary one-time compliance, store, and operational costs within a Founder-set cap | No acquisition spend or long-term minimum guarantee |

| **T3 — Release carefully** | After G3 | Essential hosting, support, privacy, monitoring, and measured experiments with a written stop rule | No spend based on unverified installs, reach, or forecast revenue |
| **T4 — Scale from receipts** | After G4 plus reconciled unit economics | Scale only if contribution after store fees, refunds, support, hosting, and acquisition is evidenced positive for the cohort being scaled | No debt, exclusivity, or minimum guarantee without separate Founder and counsel approval |

### Approval control

Until the Founder fills the cap fields, **every paid transaction, recurring subscription, advertising spend, contract, and renewal requires written, transaction-specific approval**. Record approver, purpose, maximum amount, currency, vendor, renewal/cancellation date, and receipt location in the Billing Register. Agents may not enter payment details or accept terms on the Founder’s behalf.

Founder to confirm: whether the Year 1 USD 20,000/month availability is a hard cap or planning envelope, and whether it rolls over. Still NOT IN DATA: one-off approval threshold; recurring transaction cap; emergency response cap; Year 2–3 budgets; approval channel and response SLA.

## 5. Never-fund / never-do list

- No covert/police/patrol/radar-emission detection, enforcement evasion, or feature that could invite unsafe driving.
- No guessed coordinates or unlicensed/unverified camera dataset; do not pay for a dataset until scope, rights, provenance, and update service are verified.
- No advertising on an active-drive screen or while a session is running; no ad may delay, obscure, or suppress a safety alert.
- No route-history sale, account identity collection, precise user-location export, or report-service activation without documented privacy/legal approval and security review.
- No paid user acquisition before G3 and an approved measurement plan; no spend scaled from impressions alone.
- No automatic renewal, exclusivity, minimum guarantee, debt, personal guarantee, or open-ended liability without Founder and local-counsel approval.
- No entering, pasting, storing, or requesting credentials in chat, Git, issue comments, logs, or deliverables.
- No edits, releases, signing, or operational changes to Whisco TV, Whisco Mobile, or any other app.
