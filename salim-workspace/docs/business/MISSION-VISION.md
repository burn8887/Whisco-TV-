# Salim Mission & Vision

**Version:** 1.1
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Status:** Founder-input-based; wording may be refined by Founder

## Mission

Build a Bahrain-first road-awareness companion intended to help drivers notice **known, mapped fixed road cameras** in time to respond calmly, lawfully, and safely—without distraction, evasion features, or promises of complete coverage. Any wider hazard category requires separate product, safety and legal review.

## Vision

Earn trust by making each warning’s source, uncertainty and limits clear. Grow beyond Bahrain only where reliable, lawfully usable data, local compliance, suitable support and product readiness have been demonstrated. GCC expansion is an ambition, not a claim of current availability or approval.

## Values as decision rules

1. **Safety before engagement.** We will surface a relevant warning without making the driver look away or pay; we will not put ads, purchase prompts, or interaction tasks on the active-drive screen.
2. **Truth before coverage claims.** We will identify approximate, incomplete, or stale data plainly; we will not imply that a map or camera feed is exhaustive or official unless evidence supports that exact claim.
3. **Human review before public camera changes.** We will hold community reports for moderation and use confirmations as supporting evidence; we will not call installation IDs identity or physical-presence verification.
4. **Privacy by minimization.** We will collect only information required for a disclosed feature and keep location on device where feasible; we will not sell route histories or request credentials in chat or code.
5. **Local respect and lawful scope.** We will design for Bahrain first and localize carefully for GCC communities; we will not detect police, patrols, covert enforcement, or radar emissions.

## Decision test

Before shipping a feature, ask: **Does it improve driver awareness without distracting, overstating data certainty, expanding surveillance, or requiring an unverified legal/data assumption?** If any answer is unclear, gate the feature behind review rather than silently shipping it.
