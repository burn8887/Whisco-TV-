# Salim — Hostile Diligence Memo

**Version:** 1.0
**Review date:** 2026-10-28
**Reviewer stance:** Skeptical outside investor, regulator, competing product owner, and affected user.
**Evidence base:** Phase 1–3 library, source findings and cited public sources as of the date above. No new user interviews, store access, cost ledger, legal opinion or field-accuracy audit was available.
**Disposition:** Do not treat this as a fundraising memo or external endorsement. The current plan is a set of testable hypotheses, not an investable forecast.

## Executive verdict

**Would I fund Salim today? No—not on the evidence available.** The project has two native applications and a clear set of Founder boundaries, but the most important commercial and operational claims are unproven. The available source-code review found empty static camera packs, approximate/incomplete map data, and local-only unverified community drafts. A camera alert product without demonstrably reliable, lawfully usable, current data is not a defensible product; it may be a trust liability. Free substitutes already exist, including Waze, Radarbot's free tier and Bahrain's official traffic-camera locator. User demand, product differentiation, repeat use, price, revenue, costs, legal entity, domain and moderation capacity are not evidenced.

A responsible next move is a small, no-cost or explicitly Founder-approved validation program—not an optimistic forecast, paid scale campaign, data purchase, or public coverage claim. No amount or expected income can be responsibly stated until the Founder supplies target income, spend limits, actual invoices and permission to run measured tests.

## Counter-case: plausible failure path

The app reaches a handful of testers, drivers compare it with an established free map or a government locator, and they find no clearly superior coverage. A stale or missing camera record generates complaints. A community-report feature advertised as live is actually local-only, so corrections do not reach other users. Ads reduce trust or are not eligible in the moments with inventory. Users decline a subscription because an alternate app is free. Meanwhile, legal, data, support and store costs are incurred without repeat usage, verified receipts or a Founder-set cap. This path is plausible given the current unknowns; it is not a prediction.

## What would change the verdict

A positive review requires a dated evidence package, not presentation quality:

1. Verified entity authority and approved payment recipient (G1).
2. A signed, accepted test build and accurate store/privacy declarations (G2); production access and explicit release authorization (G3).
3. At least one named country with written camera-data rights, independently checked quality/freshness and a correction process (G5).
4. Documented driver research plus a consented beta showing repeat use, measured false/stale/missed-alert rates, manageable support, and no serious distraction/privacy event.
5. Reconciled real subscription/ad receipts after refunds, fees, tax and actual variable cost (G4); positive contribution evidence before scale.
6. A complete private invoice/cash register, Founder-set caps, and a monthly budget based on actual quotes and measured load.
7. If community verification is a public differentiator: deployed service, privacy/security review, trained human moderation, incident escalation, tested backup/restore and a published correction policy.

## Required Operating Rules amendments (applied in this phase)

The following controls are added to Operating Rules v1.1, effective 2026-10-28. They clarify, and do not weaken, existing gates, safety rules or Founder-only approvals.

- **Market claims:** no TAM/SAM/SOM headline unless each denominator is dated, geographically scoped, source-linked and definition-compatible; raw vehicle registrations are not unique drivers. Do not sum non-harmonized country figures.
- **Coverage claims:** no “all GCC,” “complete,” “official,” or “verified coverage” claim without per-country written rights, defined coverage method, field-quality evidence and a dated freshness statement. A G5 pass for one source/country does not open another country.
- **Community claims:** no description of reports as shared, live, moderated, verified or visible to other users until the production path, human moderator, privacy/security controls and end-to-end evidence are verified. Local drafts must be labelled as such.
- **Revenue and forecast:** all revenue actuals are `$0` until qualifying, reconciled receipt evidence. No numeric three-year forecast or break-even claim while price, users, fees, taxes, refunds, variable cost or Founder target are missing; show conservative/realistic formulas and `NOT IN DATA`, not invented point estimates.
- **Billing and spend:** the Billing Register is an evidence ledger, not permission. No service activation, renewal, paid study, vendor, campaign or dataset commitment without Founder-specified cap and transaction-specific approval; preserve source invoices privately.
- **Research:** separate sourced desk facts, Founder reports, observed behavior and `[EST]` designs. Interview statements are not purchases, vehicle counts are not reachable users, and competitor feature pages are not local usage data.
- **Public claims and safety:** a usability result does not prove crash reduction or safer driving. No claim of safety outcome without appropriate independent evidence and review; no feature/marketing that encourages speeding or enforcement evasion.

## Final recommendation

**Proceed only with gated validation.** First close the product-truth, data-rights, entity and operational readiness gaps. Collect real user and cost evidence. Do not fund a high-growth narrative until a useful, lawful, trusted Bahrain product has repeated use and reconciled receipts. If the Founder cannot supply an affordable test budget, user access, or source rights, preserve the work and defer monetization rather than manufacture projections.
