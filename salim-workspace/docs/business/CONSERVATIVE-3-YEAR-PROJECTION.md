It is a deliberately cautious earnings case, not a recommendation to spend the whole envelope. It has no line-item allocation because vendor quotes, staffing plan, data costs, hosting architecture, marketing plan and invoices are **NOT IN DATA**. This is a simplified, cash-oriented planning projection, not an audited/statutory P&L or accounting-policy determination; a qualified accountant must confirm expense recognition, capitalization, accruals and tax treatment.

## 3. Illustrative flat-spend sensitivity (not approved beyond Year 1)

For a complete three-year downside comparison only, this sensitivity **[EST]** carries the Year 1 annualized envelope forward unchanged: USD 240,000 of operating expense in each of Years 1, 2 and 3, with USD 0 revenue. Method: repeat the supplied Year 1 annualized amount (`20,000 × 12`) for each year; no inflation, expansion, price, headcount, or growth adjustment is included. This is not a Founder-provided Year 2/3 budget, available cash commitment, or spending approval. It must be replaced when later-year budgets and actuals are known.

| USD | Year 1 | Year 2 [EST] | Year 3 [EST] | Three-year total [EST] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Operating expenses (assumes full envelope spent) | $240,000 | $240,000 | $240,000 | $720,000 |
| Founder salary | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Net operating result, before tax/financing | **($240,000)** | **($240,000)** | **($240,000)** | **($720,000)** |

**Interpretation:** this is an earnings downside scenario under full budget utilization and no receipts. It does not establish that USD 720,000 is available, required, authorized, or funded. If actual spend is lower, expense and loss reduce by the amount not spent. If real receipts occur after G4, update revenue with reconciled actuals and show conservative/realistic future bands based on measured cohorts; do not backfill unsupported revenue.

## 5. Model limits and next update

- No cash-flow statement or runway is presented: business cash balance, financing, payment timing, receivables, payables and working capital are **NOT IN DATA**.
- No taxes, indirect-tax obligations, platform commissions, refunds, depreciation, interest or one-time setup costs are forecast. These can change net result materially; get qualified Bahrain advice and current platform terms.
- No user acquisition, customer count, paid conversion, subscription price, ad yield or growth rate has been invented.
- “Conservative” means the explicit downside scenario uses no revenue and, in the sensitivity only, full use of the stated spending envelope. It is not an evidence-based probability-weighted expectation.
- Reforecast after the Founder provides Year 2/3 spending limits, target income, price and scope; and after actual cohort, settlement, supplier and staffing data exists. Preserve the zero-revenue rule until G4 evidence.
