## Short profile

Salim — Road Awareness (سالم) is a Bahrain-first product concept for helping drivers become aware of **known, mapped fixed road cameras**. It is being developed for native Android and iOS. The product is intended to expand across GCC markets only as lawful, reliable data and local requirements are validated. Camera information may be incomplete, approximate or out of date; Salim does not promise a complete or official camera register.

## About the project

Salim’s purpose is to give drivers useful advance context without encouraging speeding, evasion, distraction or unsafe interaction. The intended product scope is fixed, publicly mapped cameras only. It does not include detection of police, patrols, covert enforcement or radar emissions. A warning is informational and does not replace attention to road conditions, signs, local law or safe driving.

Salim is being built as a native mobile product for Bahrain first, with possible GCC expansion subject to country-by-country data rights, regulatory, product and operational review. Public-source map information is not a guarantee of coverage, precision, current installation status or legal classification. User-submitted camera reports are not a live feature in the current project record; any future community contribution would require prior approval, privacy review and human moderation.

## Why Salim exists

The project aims to reduce surprise and support calmer, lawful decisions by making known fixed-camera information easier to understand. It prioritizes safety over engagement, accuracy over coverage claims, data minimization, and transparent treatment of uncertainty.
